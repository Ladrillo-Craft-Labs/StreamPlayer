Privacy Policy — StreamPlayer

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Package: com.ladrillocraftlabs.streamplayer • Developer: Ladrillo Craft Labs

01. Overview

Ladrillo Craft Labs ("we," "our," or "us") built StreamPlayer (com.ladrillocraftlabs.streamplayer) as a free, lightweight media player application. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how StreamPlayer handles device permissions, local media files, and network requests when you use our application.

02. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Account Required: StreamPlayer does not require user registration, login credentials, phone numbers, or email addresses to operate.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, behavioral trackers, or invasive telemetry SDKs.

03. Android Permissions We Request and Why

  • Storage / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly on-device to scan, list, and play video and audio files stored locally on your Android device. Your local media library is never uploaded or shared externally.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to stream user-provided network URLs, IPTV playlists (M3U), and retrieve public media metadata (such as titles, posters, and episode details) from open third-party metadata APIs.
  • Wake Lock & Picture-in-Picture / Audio Focus: Used to keep the display active during video playback and support floating-window or background audio playback.

04. Third-Party Services & User-Provided Streams

StreamPlayer may query open, public metadata databases over HTTPS to display movie, series, or anime artwork and descriptions. When you open direct stream URLs, IPTV playlists, or third-party add-on endpoints, your device connects directly to those external servers. Ladrillo Craft Labs does not host, control, or log the streams you access.

05. Local Data Storage & Children's Privacy

All playback preferences, subtitle settings, and saved stream URLs are stored locally in your device's internal app storage. Uninstalling StreamPlayer or clearing app storage permanently deletes this data.

StreamPlayer does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
